How to Order

A straightforward four-step process from sequence to delivery — with a written quotation before any work begins.

Step 1

Send your requirements

Share your sequence, required quantity, purity and any modifications via the contact form or by phone.

Step 2

Receive a written quote

We return pricing and an estimated delivery date, typically within 1–2 working days. No work starts until you approve it.

Step 3

Synthesis & QC

Your peptide is synthesised, purified and quality-checked in-house, with mass spec confirmation as standard.

Step 4

Delivery

Peptides are shipped with a certificate of analysis. Fast track delivery available at approximately 25% premium.

Delivery

Peptides are generally purified as trifluoroacetate (TFA) salts and supplied as a lyophilised powder. HCl or acetate salts can be requested at the quotation stage.

QC data supplied

Every order ships with LCMS data — RP-HPLC and electrospray mass spectrometry — plus a safety datasheet.

Shipping

Standard delivery is usually 3–5 weeks from receipt of order, by courier at room temperature. Cold-chain shipping is available on request.

Delivery & shipping

Orders are shipped within the UK and internationally, packaged according to the stability requirements of your peptide (including cold-chain shipping where necessary). Delivery timescales are confirmed at the quotation stage and depend on sequence length, purity and whether fast track has been requested.
